Before you sign — read this

• Gas: every signature and tip is an on-chain transaction on BNB Smart Chain. You pay the gas, and you confirm every transaction in your own wallet. Nothing is ever signed automatically.

• Tips are irreversible. A tip sent to the wrong name or address cannot be undone. Double-check the recipient before confirming.

• No returns, no earnings. AgentWall is a guestbook, not an investment. Nothing here earns, yields, or promises any return.

• Owner permission (disclosed): the contract owner can withdraw the BNB that accumulates in the contract from guestbook signature tips, via withdrawTips(). That is the only owner-only function. The owner cannot touch your wallet, change the Agent ID contract, or move funds you did not already send to the contract. The contract source is open and verified on BscScan.

• Your keys stay yours. This page never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or password, and never asks you to scan a QR code to log in.
